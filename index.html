Your Money is Safe

Escrow Protection
for Every Payment Made in the App

In traditional livestock trading, sending money to a stranger is a leap of faith. Garabaly takes that risk off the table for every payment made in the app, with escrow split per party, encryption and fraud checks. One escrow hold, whether the order stands alone or is bundled: nothing is paid out until you enter your code. Then the providers are paid at once, and the seller's share enters a 24-hour buyer-protection window.

Have questions about how escrow works? → See our FAQ

Confirmation Code System

One 6-character code per order — even for bundles with several parties. It is encrypted and protected against repeated guessing. For escrow orders, the code stays hidden from the seller and providers until you confirm receipt. One code completes the order and all its service bookings at once.

24-Hour Buyer Protection Window

Nothing is released until you enter your code. The moment you do, the service providers (butcher, vet, delivery) are paid and the seller's payment enters a 24-hour window — during which you can open a dispute if something is wrong. The 24-hour window applies only to the seller's share.

Fraud Detection & Security

Spending limits, device checks and real-time monitoring watch over every payment in the app. Your wallet is secured with a private PIN and SMS verification.

Full Transparency

Buyer, seller and providers all see where the escrow stands, with live status updates. A countdown shows the time left in the 24-hour dispute window. Cash and escrow orders are clearly labeled. Every franc paid through the app is recorded and accounted for.
